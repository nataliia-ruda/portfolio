JobFit Analyzer

JobFit Analyzer lets you paste a job posting and upload your CV to instantly get an AI-generated match analysis. It returns a fit score, a breakdown of skill gaps (required vs. present in your CV), and concrete, actionable suggestions for improving your CV against that specific role.

Features

  • Upload a CV (PDF) and paste any job description.
  • AI analyzes the match using OpenAI's GPT-4o-mini with structured, schema-validated output.
  • Returns a numeric match score with color-coded feedback (green/amber/red).
  • Lists skill gaps, flagging which are required vs. preferred, and whether each is present in the CV.
  • Gives targeted CV improvement suggestions per skill area.
  • Clean drag-and-drop upload interface.

Technologies

  • React
  • TypeScript
  • Material UI
  • Node.js
  • Express
